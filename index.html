Scoring & Decision Rules

The dashboard separates candidacy from economics so salary never quietly corrupts fit.

Traffic light: GREEN = strong enough candidacy to apply; YELLOW = selective reach; RED = hard gate / poor screen / no résumé labor. Screening Fit estimates résumé-screen probability against actual job description gates. Capability Fit estimates ability to perform the substantive work. Office Cuteness is separate; unknown stays “-” rather than being invented. Suggested Ask is negotiation guidance, not fit. Zombie: a pending application automatically becomes Zombie when the browser date reaches 10 U.S. business days after its recorded application date; weekends and U.S. federal holidays are excluded. Pending records without a recovered application date cannot auto-Zombie. Live posting status: tracked openings are periodically rechecked and marked open, closed, or needing confirmation.
